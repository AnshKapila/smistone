#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Overused Typography**: Fraunces and Inter are standard fallbacks. The brand could use a more distinct architectural serif or geometric sans. Fix: Swap out Fraunces/Inter for something with more unique character. Suggested command: `$impeccable typeset`
